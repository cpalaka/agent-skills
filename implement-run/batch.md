## Inside a batch

**Inside a batch.** A brief that states the owner's delegation makes this run one ticket of a
delegated batch, dispatched by the delegate, the main session standing in for the owner. The shape
is `subagents` whatever the knob says: the Workflow tool is absent at depth 1, and a pane driven
from a subagent is unmeasured. Every stop this Skill gives the owner goes to the delegate, handed
back in the form the brief names: `STOP <kind>`, nothing pending (`implement-batch` § Hand-back and
resume, read only where that Skill's directory exists under `~/.claude/skills` or
`~/.agents/skills`). The first hand-back carries the plan and the profile block as posted. A
`coordinator` dispatch takes its role from its definition's pin and the meter from the delegate's
read, so neither is asked. A herdr pane child whose
prompt states the delegation, the fallback transport, is a main session instead: it reads its own
role and meter as § Start and § Advisor slots say, and a Planner pane asks the delegate, which
parks it.

The delegate's stops are a closed set: the plan stop, where it declines every recommended add-on,
the ticket running the default and the record naming each, while a pin, the owner's decision at
filing, still applies; the Close approval; the two cap stops (a round past `fix rounds`, no add-on,
answered on its merits; work past the plan's deliverable count), each handed back as `STOP plan`,
since a cap raise is a plan pin, which fires the plan stop (§ Run profile); and `STOP slot-3` on
either of two preconditions — where an advisor runs, a slot-3 need after the advisor, for only what
the advisor cannot settle; where none runs (the `advisor` dial off, or the advisor unavailable, as
§ Advisor slots' Fallback lists), a false premise met mid-run whose disposition leaves every
acceptance criterion satisfied in form with the failed premise named. (Where an advisor runs, that
false premise goes to it first.) Every other stop is the owner's and is `STOP park`: a re-cost, a
judgment the project's contract reserves to the owner, a `gate:decide`-shaped question, a gated
write outside the grant.

A gated write the grant names is no stop: the grant is its approval, and the run record names that
write under `Slots` beside the grant. The grant waives no gate: the verify gate, the force-push and
`--no-verify` bans and `git-flow-squash`'s pre-push audit stay in force. Load `SKILL.md` by path:
the Skill tool's refusal of a slash-only Skill addresses a session replicating the workflow for
itself, not a coordinator the owner delegated.
